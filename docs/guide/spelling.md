# Spelling

This page is for the person who writes the notes. It covers the spell check of
the editor.

## What the spell check marks

Your browser checks the spelling of a note while you write. It underlines a
word that it does not know. Right-click the word to see the suggestions of your
browser, or to add the word to its dictionary.

The spell check skips text that is not prose: code, a web address, HTML, the
front matter at the top of a note, and math. The text of a link and of an
image description stays in the check.

## Languages

Your browser chooses the languages and holds the dictionary. To check a
different language, add that language to the spell check settings of your
browser. A browser with its spell check turned off shows no marks.

tidy notebook does not check grammar. Some browsers can check grammar with the
same marks when you turn that on in the browser.

## Turn the spell check off

1. Open **Settings** and go to **Spelling**.
2. Clear **Check spelling**.

The choice goes to your account, so your other devices follow it.
