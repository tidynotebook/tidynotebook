# Joining an installation

This page is for a person who joins a tidy notebook installation, with an
invitation or with a sign-up. It also shows how to reset a password and how to
delete your account.

## Accept an invitation

An administrator of the installation sends you a link, by mail or in a
message.

1. Open the link. The page shows the email address of your new account. You
   cannot change it.
2. Enter your name if you want to. An administrator sees it. You can leave it
   empty.
3. Enter a password of at least 15 characters, and enter it again. The
   application refuses a password that appears in a list of leaked passwords.
4. If the installation has terms and a privacy notice, the page asks you to
   accept them. The words **terms** and **privacy notice** are links. Read
   them, then select **I accept the terms and the privacy notice**.
5. Select **Make account**.

Your account opens at once. It has its own workspace and one note, **Welcome
to tidy notebook**. See [Getting started](getting-started.md) for the first
steps.

Every later visit shows **Sign in**. Use the same address and password.

If the page says that the invitation is not valid, the link expired, somebody
used it, or an administrator revoked it. Ask the administrator for a new link.

## Sign up without an invitation

Some installations let anybody make an account. The sign-in screen then shows
**Create an account**.

1. Select **Create an account**.
2. Enter your email address and a password of at least 15 characters.
3. Open the **terms** and the **privacy notice** links next to the check box,
   and read them. Then select **I accept the terms and the privacy notice**.
4. Select **Create account**. The browser does a short check, and a message
   goes to your address.
5. Open the link in the message before it expires. The message states the
   time. The page says **Your account is ready**.
6. Sign in with your address and your password.

Until you open the link, a sign-in says that the address is not proved yet. If
the message does not come, look in your spam folder. You can also sign up
again to get a new link.

## Forgot your password

1. Select **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen.
2. Enter the address of your account, and select **Send link**.
3. Open the link in the message within 1 hour. The link works once.
4. Enter a new password twice, and select **Save password**.

Every device that was signed in to your account signs out. A message tells you
that the password changed. Sign in again with the new password.

If you get that message and you did not change the password, reset it at once
and tell an administrator.

Some installations send no mail, so they show no **Forgot password**. Ask the
person who runs the server to recover your account. That person finds the
command in [Server operations](operations.md#account-commands).

## Who can read your notes

Your account owns every note in your workspaces. No other account can read
them, and that includes an administrator. The person who runs the server can
read every note. See [Security model](security-model.md).

**Settings** shows how much space your notes use and how much space you have.
It also links the terms and the privacy notice of the installation.

## Delete your account

1. Export your notes first if you want to keep them. See [Export](export.md).
2. Open the avatar menu at the right end of the header, and select
   **Settings**.
3. In the **Delete account** section, select **Delete my account**.
4. Enter your password to confirm.

Your account and every note in your workspaces go for good.
